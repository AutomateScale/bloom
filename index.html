🌻 Bloom

A calm home for the hardest fight.

When your child is seriously ill, the information becomes its own crisis — scattered across inboxes, patient portals, and notebooks at 3 a.m. Bloom pulls it all into one clear, private place, so you can spend your energy on your child instead of the chaos.

Why it exists

Bloom was built by a father, in the middle of the fight — coordinating his young son's relapsed Ewing sarcoma care across three continents, dozens of clinical trials, and a mountain of scans and reports.

No tool existed for that. So he built one — and he's giving it to every family who comes next, for free.

Private by design

Your family's data stays yours.

Bloom runs in your browser and keeps your information on your own device. No accounts. No servers collecting your child's diagnosis. No tracking. Because it's open source under the MIT license, anyone can inspect exactly what it does — and adapt it.

  • Local-first — data lives on your device
  • No sign-up — open it and start
  • No tracking — no analytics, no ads
  • Open source (MIT) — free to copy, adapt, share
  • Works offline — a single file that just works

For every family

Built for Ewing sarcoma — made to help anyone.

It started with one rare pediatric cancer, but the chaos is the same for every serious diagnosis. Bloom is designed so any family — any disease — can pick it up and make it their own.

Bring your own AI

Turn any AI into your family's war‑room.

Bloom ships with a ready-made Copilot prompt — paste it into Claude or ChatGPT together with your Bloom data, and the AI becomes a rigorous research-and-coordination partner: finding and vetting clinical trials, summarizing doctor replies, and prepping your questions for the next visit. The same "war room" a parent built during his own child's fight — packaged for you.

Also: a tiny command-line tool
$ bloom trials "Ewing sarcoma" $ bloom copilot # print the AI prompt $ bloom open # launch the app

Pure Python 3 — no install, no dependencies. Searches ClinicalTrials.gov right from your terminal.
