# Bloom 🌱

**A free, private, open-source toolkit for any parent fighting for a seriously ill child.**

When your child is diagnosed with something grave, you are suddenly drowning — in scans, pathology reports, doctor emails, trial names, second opinions, and impossible decisions — in one of the hardest moments a family can face. This toolkit was born from one family's fight. It exists so no parent has to build the system from scratch while the clock is running.

It gives you:
- **A war-room board** — the whole situation on one screen: what's happening, what's next, what's overdue.
- **An outreach tracker** — every expert, trial, and foundation you contact, and who has replied. Runs in your browser, saves locally.
- **A knowledge base ("second brain")** — one organized place for every report and record, so an AI assistant can answer *"what did the last scan show?"* or *"who haven't we heard back from?"* grounded in your real documents.
- **Report templates** — a doctor-visit question guide, a master case summary, and an outreach-email set you adapt to your child.

## 🔒 Privacy first — this is the whole point
Your child's medical data is the most sensitive information you will ever hold. So:
- **Everything runs on *your* computer.** There is no server, no account, no cloud that we control. We never see your data. Nobody does.
- The tracker saves to **your browser's local storage**. The reports live in **your folders** or **your own** Google Drive.
- This public project contains **zero personal data** — only blank tools and templates. You fill in your child's information privately, and it stays with you.

## 🚀 Quick start (non-technical parents welcome)
1. Download this folder.
2. Double-click **`index.html`** to read the friendly guide, or go straight to the app: double-click **`app.html`**.
3. In the app, click **"Connect a folder"** and choose the folder where you keep your child's records (on your computer, or your Google Drive desktop folder). It reads them **privately, on your machine** — nothing is uploaded.
4. Fill in the child profile, use the **Questions to Ask** guide, track your **Outreach**, and click **AI Assist** to generate a ready-to-paste briefing for an assistant like Claude.

**The app (`app.html`)** is the heart of it: a single self-contained page that reads your document folder locally, organizes it, tells you what to ask, tracks your outreach, and builds AI briefings from your own data — all with zero data ever leaving your computer.
